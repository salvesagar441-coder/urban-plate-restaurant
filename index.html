Our Story

Where Every Plate Tells a Story

Founded in 2017, Urban Plate was born from a simple belief: great food starts with great ingredients. What began as a small neighborhood bistro on Elm Street has grown into one of the city's most beloved dining destinations.

Our Executive Chef, Marcus Rivera, brings over 18 years of culinary experience from kitchens across New York, San Francisco, and Barcelona. His approach is rooted in seasonality — every dish on our menu reflects what's freshest from local farms and purveyors we've partnered with for years.

We don't just serve meals. We craft experiences — whether it's a Tuesday night dinner with friends or a milestone celebration. Our warm, design-forward space was made to feel like an extension of your own home, just with better lighting and a considerably better wine list.
